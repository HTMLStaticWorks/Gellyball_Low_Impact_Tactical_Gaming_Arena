Our Origins

Founded in 2024 by tactical gaming enthusiasts, our arena was built with one goal: to provide the ultimate low-impact combat experience. We realized paintball was too painful for younger players, and laser tag lacked the visceral feel of a real projectile.

Enter Gellyball. We transformed an abandoned warehouse into a 20,000 sq ft neon-lit combat grid, complete with modular bunkers, sniper towers, and UV environments.

Our Mission

To deliver heart-pounding, adrenaline-fueled entertainment in a safe, inclusive, and highly immersive environment. We strive to create unforgettable memories for players of all ages, fostering teamwork, communication, and sportsmanship.

Our Vision

To become the premier destination for tactical gaming entertainment nationwide, pioneering new technologies in the Gellyball industry and continually evolving our arenas to offer next-generation combat simulations.
