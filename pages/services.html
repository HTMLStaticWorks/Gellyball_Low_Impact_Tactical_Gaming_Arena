Intel / FAQ

Does Gellyball hurt?

Not at all! It feels like a very light rubber band snap. The gellyballs burst on impact and are mostly water, making it safe for ages 5+.

What should I wear?

Comfortable athletic clothing and closed-toe shoes are required. Gellyballs are non-staining and leave no mess, so you won't ruin your clothes.
