Mastering the Surge Blaster: Pro Tips

Marcus Kane Oct 15, 2026

The Standard Issue Powerhouse

The Surge Blaster X1 is the backbone of our armory. While many players look immediately to the Pro upgrades, mastering the standard issue blaster is the true mark of a veteran. Here is how you can dominate the arena without spending an extra dime.

Burst Firing is Key

Unlike paintballs, gellyballs are incredibly light. If you hold down the trigger for a full auto spray, the air turbulence from the rapid fire will cause the balls to spread wildly. Instead, use controlled 3-5 round bursts.

"Amateurs spray and pray. Veterans control their fire and secure the objective." - Sarah Vance, Head Ref
